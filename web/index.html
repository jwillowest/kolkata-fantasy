Draft vs ADP

ADP = FantasyPros expert consensus rank just before our draft (PPR; we score half-PPR). Points = regular-season fantasy points in our scoring, wherever the player ended up. Steal / reach = pick minus ADP: + means taken later than consensus (D/ST, K and keepers left out). Pick value = points minus what that slot usually returns at the position: the 5th RB taken is measured against what consensus RB3–RB7 scored that season. Beat ADP = share of picks who outscored what their ADP implied.
